01 / Overview

Bridging the analytical rigor of courtroom litigation with structural excellence in corporate governance and enterprise administration.

Sanju Maharjan is an independent-thinking legal professional based in Kathmandu. Serving as Company Secretary at Business Interest Group of Industries Ltd, she administers vital corporate documentation, directs compliance frameworks, and coordinates transparent shareholder relations.
