Trained in litigation, now building in corporate governance — where legal precision meets the everyday running of a company.

Sanju is a lawyer based in Kathmandu, currently serving as Company Secretary at Business Interest Group of Industries Ltd, where she manages statutory compliance, shareholder relations and corporate records. She is also the co-founder of Nyayik Paridhi, a legal initiative she has run since 2023.

Her path began in litigation and public interest work — as a Judicial Facilitator with Kathmandu Metropolitan City and a legal intern with the Public Defender's Society, Nepal — before moving into corporate practice at Pradhan & Associates and, since 2026, into company secretarial work.
