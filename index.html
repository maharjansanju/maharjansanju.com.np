Corporate Governance & Minority Shareholder Protections Under the Companies Act of Nepal

Abstract

This paper examines the statutory checks and balances established under the prevailing Companies Act of Nepal to mitigate oppression of minority shareholders. Analyzing judicial precedents and corporate registry filing protocols, it addresses structural ambiguities in board resolution validity, OTC share transfer impediments, and judicial remedies available before the Commercial Bench.

Local Government & ADR Working Paper • 2024
Restricted PDF

Municipal Judicial Committees: Conciliation Frameworks and Grassroots Justice in Kathmandu

Abstract

Drawing on first-hand empirical experience in municipal judicial facilitation, this study analyzes the functioning of Local Government Judicial Committees under the Local Government Operation Act 2074. It assesses the balance between formal legal procedure and community mediation in land disputes, boundary encroachments, and household conflicts within Kathmandu Metropolitan City.
